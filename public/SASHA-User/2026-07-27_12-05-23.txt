  2. THE MAISON / OUR STORYOpening
  
  Maison Orelisse began with the conviction that the most meaningful products
  often emerge from careful observation rather than trend.
  
  A dermatologist sees skin not in isolation but as a responsive organ in
  continuous contact with the world. Light, climate, travel, cleansing,
  friction, occupation, and habit all leave their mark.
  
  The Maison was created to explore those points of contact and to develop
  more considered ways of caring for them.
  A Broader View of Care
  
  Orelisse is not limited to a single category.
  
  Its work may take the form of a skincare formula, a travel ritual, a
  sun-protective textile, or a grooming product for a companion animal. What
  connects these expressions is a shared method: identify a real need,
  understand the skin involved, and design the complete experience with
  intention.
  The Founder
  
  Eric Wyatt, MD, is a board-certified dermatologist with decades of
  experience caring for skin.
  
  Maison Orelisse reflects both his medical background and his belief that
  effective care must also be practical, elegant, and compatible with daily
  life.
  
  The Maison is an independent, evolving project built around curiosity,
  discipline, and a respect for the living barrier between ourselves and the
  world.
  ------------------------------
  3. THE ORELISSE METHODIntroduction
  
  The Orelisse Method is the process through which observation becomes
  formulation and formulation becomes ritual.
  
  It provides a consistent framework across every area of the Maison.
  Observe
  
  We begin with how skin is actually experienced.
  
  Where does discomfort occur? What is repeatedly overlooked? How does the
  environment alter the way a product must perform? What prevents an
  otherwise useful ritual from becoming part of everyday life?
  Formulate
  
  Ingredients are selected for a defined purpose within the formula.
  
  The objective is not to build the longest ingredient list or follow the
  latest trend. It is to create a coherent formulation in which each
  component contributes to performance, comfort, stability, or experience.
  Refine
  
  A formula cannot be separated from the way it is used.
  
  Texture, delivery, packaging, application, residue, portability, and visual
  character are refined alongside the formulation itself.
  Ritualize
  
  The best product is one that becomes intuitive to use.
  
  Orelisse products are designed to fit naturally into repeated moments of
  care—at home, in transit, outdoors, or alongside the animals who share our
  lives.
  Closing
  
  *From observation to ritual.*
  ------------------------------
  4. THE SCIENCEIntroduction
  
  Science at Maison Orelisse is not used as decoration.
  
  It is a method of asking better questions, evaluating materials carefully,
  and distinguishing plausible benefit from unsupported promise.
  Skin as a Living Barrier
  
  The skin barrier helps regulate the relationship between the body and its
  surroundings. Its condition may be influenced by cleansing, climate,
  ultraviolet exposure, friction, travel, dryness, and repeated environmental
  stress.
  
  Our development process pays particular attention to barrier support,
  tolerability, hydration, practical protection, and the sensory qualities
  that affect consistent use.
  Evidence with Restraint
  
  Scientific language should clarify rather than overwhelm.
  
  Maison Orelisse will communicate ingredient functions and product benefits
  in proportion to the available evidence. We will avoid exaggerated claims
  and will distinguish between formulation rationale, testing results, and
  established clinical evidence.
  Development
  
  Products may involve collaboration with cosmetic chemists, textile
  specialists, veterinarians, manufacturers, regulatory consultants, and
  other technical partners appropriate to the category.
  
  Only verified testing, certifications, and performance claims should be
  published.
  ------------------------------
  5. INGREDIENT PHILOSOPHYIntroduction
  
  Ingredients matter, but context matters just as much.
  
  An ingredient’s value depends upon its form, concentration, compatibility,
  stability, delivery system, and relationship to the complete formula.
  Formulation Over Fashion
  
  Maison Orelisse does not select ingredients simply because they are
  fashionable or unfamiliar.
  
  We look for materials that serve a defined purpose: cleansing gently,
  supporting hydration, improving condition, contributing to barrier care,
  enhancing application, or protecting the integrity of the formulation.
  Sensory Experience Is Functional
  
  Texture, spread, absorption, residue, fragrance, and packaging all
  influence whether a product is used consistently.

  performance rather than as an afterthought.
  Responsible Claims

  “chemical-free,” or universally safe.
  
  Claims will be specific, supportable, and appropriate to the product
  category.

  6. THE ORELISSE WORLD
  
  This page should introduce the different expressions of the Maison without
  making the site feel fragmented.
  Introductory Copy
  
  One philosophy may take many forms.
  
  The Orelisse world extends across human skincare, travel, sun protection,
  textiles, and companion-animal care. Each project responds to a different
  environment, but all begin with the same attention to skin, material, use,
  and ritual.
  PawVellé by Orelisse
  
  PawVellé began with a moment its founder could not ignore: watching his own
  dog persistently chewing and licking at his paws.
  
  As a dermatologist, he understood the importance of caring for skin with
  intention—not only cleansing, but hydrating, conditioning, and supporting
  the skin barrier.
  
  After seeing little visible improvement with conventional grooming
  products, he set out to bring a more thoughtful, dermatologist-crafted
  approach to pet skin and coat care.
  
  PawVellé was created from the belief that pets deserve the same level of
  care, refinement, and ingredient intention we expect from the best of
  modern skincare.
  Rituels
  
  Rituels explores skincare for moments of transition and environmental
  change, particularly travel.
  
  The collection is being developed around hydration, skin-barrier comfort,
  elegant portability, and products that remain practical in real conditions
  rather than only in the bathroom at home.
  
  *In development*
  Soleil
  
  Soleil explores intelligent protection for people whose lives and
  occupations involve meaningful ultraviolet exposure.
  
  Concepts may include refined topical formats, lip care, portable
  reapplication products, and professional-looking UV-protective apparel.
  
  *In development*
