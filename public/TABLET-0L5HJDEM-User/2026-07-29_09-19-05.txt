  nteractive Brokers operates the MCP server. You log in through IBKR’s own authentication screen and authorize access. IBKR says your brokerage password and API keys are not passed to the AI provider. You can later revoke the connection through Settings → Manage Third-Party Consents.
  
  This differs from older trading-bot setups in which users might give a third-party application an API key capable of directly placing orders.
  
  What information the AI can access
  
  Depending on the authorization, account permissions and market-data entitlements, the AI can work with information such as:
  
  Current positions and cash balances
  Buying power and margin availability
  Realized and unrealized profit or loss
  Historical transactions and recent trades
  Open orders and execution status
  Option chains
  Portfolio and concentration risks
  Multi-currency balances
  Linked-account and portfolio structures
  Current or delayed market data
  
  Interactive Brokers states that market-data retrieval remains subject to the client’s existing data entitlements. Therefore, connecting an AI does not automatically provide every real-time exchange feed for free.
