  # NEXAI Website — Phase 4: Benefits Section Only
  
  Continuing the section-by-section build. Hero, About, and What We Do
  are approved. This phase covers **only** the Benefits section. Do not
  build Tokenomics, Roadmap, Team, or Footer yet.
  
  ## Purpose of This Section
  
  About made the case for why this is a problem; What We Do explained
  the mechanism. This section answers "why this specifically, over the
  alternatives" — the real differentiators.
  
  ## Content Direction
  
  Core benefits to cover, per the original concept doc positioning:
  - **Non-custodial** — you hold the token, nobody holds your assets
    for you
  - **No leverage, no derivatives** — real backing, not synthetic
    exposure with liquidation risk
  - **Globally accessible** — no accreditation requirement, no
    $200K+ income gate like traditional AI-sector funds
  - **Actively funds the ecosystem** — a share of protocol fees goes to
    an AI Grants Fund, which governance token holders vote to allocate
    toward promising AI/crypto projects. This isn't just passive
    holding — NEXAI is meant to feel like it's actively supporting the
    sector it tracks, not just riding it
  
  Consider a direct, brief comparison against the alternatives already
  established in About (single-token bets, manual multi-token
  management, accreditation-gated funds) — but don't just repeat About's
  content, frame it as "here's how NEXAI specifically avoids each of
  those problems."
  
  ## Suggested Visual Direction (propose your own take)
  
  A 4-item benefit layout (cards, a grid, or a list — your call) is a
  reasonable default here, but per the standing design mandate, avoid
  this reading as the generic "three/four icon cards in a row" template
  seen everywhere. Consider: does each benefit deserve a small, specific
  visual (not generic checkmark/shield icons), or is a more editorial/
  text-forward treatment more fitting for this section given Hero, About,
  and What We Do have already carried the heavier visual weight? Your
  call — but make it deliberate, not a default grid.
  
  ## Explicitly Out of Scope for This Phase
  
  - Tokenomics, Roadmap, Team, Footer — later phases
