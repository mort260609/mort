  First, I want to say that we're extremely happy with the direction of the brand. The logo, typography, colors, and overall visual identity are exactly where we hoped they would be. The foundation is strong.

  A design principle kept emerging throughout our review:
  
  The brand communicates quite confidence through restraint. When given the choice between adding another element or removing one, we'd almost always prefer to simplify. White space is part of the brand.
  
  With that in mind, here are our comments.
  
  Overall
  We consider the identity itself complete.
  The logo system, wordmark, colors, typography, and symbol are approved.
  We'd like future applications to trust the identity more and rely less on additional messaging or decorative elements.
  Business Card

  Letterhead
  We really like the header.
  We'd like to remove the footer entirely rather than simplifying it.
  The header already establishes the brand well, and we think the document feels stronger without additional messaging or graphics at the bottom.
  Envelopes
  
  We'd like to simplify these significantly.
  
  Front
  
  Keep the logo.
  Keep the bottom accent line.
  Remove all other contact information and messaging.
  
  Back
  
  Keep only the bottom accent line.
  Remove the tagline and other branding.
  LinkedIn / Marketing Graphics
  
  We think these should move away from generic business imagery and toward a visual language that's uniquely EssentialCFO.

  oversized

  subtle pattern
  design anchor
  
  The symbol is becoming a recognizable part of the identity in its own right, and we'd like to lean into that.
