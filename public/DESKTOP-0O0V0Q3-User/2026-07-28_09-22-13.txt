  GORILLA 4444
  AUTOMATED TV SUBSCRIPTION
  & ACTIVATION PLATFORM
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Professional Product & Development Specification
  SCAN  →  SELECT  →  PAY  →  WATCH
  
  Prepared for David Nixon
  Version 1.0  |  28 July 2026
  Development Brief
  
  Document Purpose
  This document converts the original Gorilla 4444 concept notes into a structured product and development specification suitable for discussion with software developers, technical partners and potential investors.
  Core proposition
  Gorilla 4444 is not simply a television application or a sales website. It is intended to be a fully automated, self-service subscription platform in which a customer scans a QR code, chooses a package, pays online and has the service activated automatically on the original television or device.
  
  1. Executive Summary
  Gorilla 4444 is designed to remove the manual work normally involved in selling and activating a television subscription. The platform will combine a customer-facing TV application, a mobile payment journey, reseller controls, a master administration panel and direct integration with the external service-management platform.
  The intended customer experience is deliberately simple:
  1  SCAN
  QR code on TV	2  SELECT
  Subscription package	3  PAY
  Recurring PayPal checkout	4  WATCH
  Automatic activation
  
  Once payment is confirmed, the backend will create or extend the relevant subscription through the MegaOTT Panel API and securely associate it with the device that generated the QR code. The customer should not need to enter a portal URL, username, password or other technical configuration.
  2. Project Background and Current Position
  ⦁	An earlier version of the concept was started with a developer but was not completed.
  ⦁	A supporting website was created at www.cherrygold.be to explain the original concept.
  ⦁	No usable source code, application repository or technical handover is currently available to the project owner.
  ⦁	Unless the previous code can be recovered and independently verified, the project should be planned as a fresh build.
  ⦁	The external service-management platform exposes an API for authenticated user and subscription operations.
  Important development assumption
  The new developer must not assume that any earlier code, database, application package or deployment environment can be reused. All required source code, documentation, credentials and deployment ownership must be delivered under the new project.
  
  3. Product Vision and Objectives
  Objective	Required Outcome
  Automation	Eliminate routine manual payment collection, account creation, credential entry, activation and renewal work.
  Simplicity	Enable a non-technical customer to begin from the TV screen and complete the entire purchase on a mobile phone.
  Speed	Move from payment confirmation to active television service with minimal delay and clear on-screen status updates.
  Control	Give the platform owner central control and give each reseller access only to their own customers and permitted settings.
  Scalability	Support growth from a small customer base to thousands of customers without a matching increase in administrative staff.
  Brand quality	Provide a polished, fast and remote-control-friendly interface comparable in usability to leading TV applications, while maintaining an original Gorilla 4444 design.
  Commercial flexibility	Allow different packages, recurring billing, reseller operations and a future white-label version for other providers.
  
  4. Platform Structure
  Component	Purpose
  Master Administration Panel	Restricted to the platform owner. Controls global platform configuration, API connections, portal details, packages, resellers, reporting and system-wide settings.
  Reseller Panel	Allows an authorised reseller to add and manage customers, monitor subscriptions and payments, issue application download links and perform permitted support actions.
  Customer TV Application	Displays the QR code, pairing status, subscription status and the full television interface after activation.
  Mobile Purchase Journey	Opens after QR scanning and handles package selection, customer details, recurring PayPal payment and confirmation.
  Automation Backend	Connects the application, payment gateway, database and MegaOTT API. It controls activation, renewal, suspension, logging and exception handling.
  Operations Database	Stores customers, devices, resellers, packages, payments, subscriptions, identifiers, status history and audit records.
  
  5. End-to-End Customer Journey
  1.	The reseller creates or invites a customer and sends the approved Gorilla 4444 application download link.
  2.	The customer installs and opens the application on the television or supported device.
  3.	The application creates a secure, short-lived pairing session and displays a unique QR code.
  4.	The customer scans the QR code with a mobile phone. A secure mobile page opens and identifies the waiting device.
  5.	The customer selects a subscription package and enters the minimum information required for billing and account management.
  6.	The customer approves a recurring subscription through PayPal Checkout. Eligible card-payment options may be presented by PayPal according to the account and market.
  7.	The Gorilla 4444 backend receives and verifies the payment notification.
  8.	The backend creates a new subscription or extends the existing subscription through the MegaOTT API.
  9.	The backend links the resulting subscription identifier and playback credentials to the original device session.
  10.	The television application receives confirmation, removes the QR/payment screen and opens the active TV interface.
  11.	Future successful recurring payments extend the service automatically. Failed, cancelled or reversed payments trigger the defined grace, warning or suspension process.
  Customer experience requirement
  The customer must never be expected to type long server addresses, usernames, passwords or configuration strings using a television remote control. The QR pairing process exists specifically to remove this friction.
  
  6. Functional Requirements
  6.1 Master Administration Panel
  ⦁	Secure owner-only login with multi-factor authentication capability.
  ⦁	Configure the MegaOTT API base URL, bearer token and relevant package/template mappings.
  ⦁	Configure portal URLs, application settings, branding, customer support details and system notices.
  ⦁	Create, suspend, edit and remove reseller accounts.
  ⦁	Set reseller permissions, commercial terms, package availability and customer limits.
  ⦁	Create and manage customer subscription packages, durations, currencies and recurring prices.
  ⦁	View customers, devices, active subscriptions, expiring subscriptions, failed payments and reseller performance.
  ⦁	Perform controlled manual actions, including activation, extension, suspension, reactivation and device reassignment.
  ⦁	View complete payment and API event logs, including failed actions and retry status.
  ⦁	Export operational reports in a standard format such as CSV.
  6.2 Reseller Panel
  ⦁	Secure reseller login and password recovery.
  ⦁	Dashboard showing customer totals, active subscriptions, upcoming expiries and payment issues.
  ⦁	Add a customer manually or create an invitation/onboarding link.
  ⦁	Send or copy the correct TV application download link and installation instructions.
  ⦁	View and manage only customers assigned to that reseller.
  ⦁	View customer device, subscription package, start date, expiry date, payment status and service status.
  ⦁	Perform permitted customer actions defined by the master administrator.
  ⦁	Record support notes without exposing protected platform API credentials.
  ⦁	Search and filter customers by name, contact details, device, status or expiry.
  6.3 Customer TV Application
  ⦁	Generate and display a unique QR code when the device is not activated.
  ⦁	Display simple instructions explaining how to scan and pay.
  ⦁	Show real-time states such as Waiting for scan, Package selected, Payment pending, Activating and Active.
  ⦁	Refresh the pairing session securely if the QR code expires.
  ⦁	Open the full television interface immediately after confirmed activation.
  ⦁	Retain the device identity securely so the customer does not repeat setup unnecessarily.
  ⦁	Display subscription status, renewal date and suitable payment/support instructions when required.
  ⦁	Support remote-control navigation, back-button behaviour and focus states consistently.
  ⦁	Recover gracefully after loss of internet connection, API delay or application restart.
  6.4 Mobile Purchase and Customer Account
  ⦁	Open from the QR code without requiring the customer to manually enter a pairing code.
  ⦁	Display the identified device and reseller/brand context.
  ⦁	Present available packages, billing frequency, currency and recurring charge clearly.
  ⦁	Collect only necessary customer details and obtain acceptance of applicable terms.
  ⦁	Create the PayPal subscription and show a clear successful, pending or failed result.
  ⦁	Allow the customer to view subscription information and obtain instructions for cancellation or payment updates.
  ⦁	Prevent a reused, expired or altered QR session from activating the wrong device.
  6.5 Automated Subscription Management
  ⦁	Create a subscription through the MegaOTT API after verified initial payment.
  ⦁	Store the returned subscription ID and relevant expiry information.
  ⦁	Extend an existing subscription after each verified recurring payment.
  ⦁	Deactivate or suspend service according to the approved failed-payment policy.
  ⦁	Reactivate service automatically after a successful recovery payment when appropriate.
  ⦁	Use retry-safe processing so the same payment notification cannot create or extend a subscription twice.
  ⦁	Place failed API actions into an exception queue for automatic retry and administrator review.
  7. Television Interface Requirements
  The television interface is a central part of the product and must not be treated as a secondary screen. It should feel like a modern streaming platform, be visually attractive and remain easy to use with a standard television remote control.
  Screen	Minimum Capability
  Home	Featured content, recently watched items, favourites, recommendations or curated categories.
  Live TV	Category navigation, channel list, programme information and electronic programme guide where available.
  Movies	Poster-led browsing, categories, search, details, resume and favourites.
  Series	Series details, seasons, episodes, continue watching and favourites.
  Search	Fast search across live channels, films and series with remote-friendly input.
  My Content	Favourites, history and continue-watching items.
  Account	Subscription status, renewal date, device information and support details.
  Settings	Language, playback preferences, parental controls where applicable and diagnostic information.
  
  Design principles
  ⦁	Original Gorilla 4444 visual identity; the interface may take usability inspiration from established applications but must not copy protected layouts or assets.
  ⦁	Large, legible typography and clear focus indicators suitable for viewing at television distance.
  ⦁	Fast initial loading, responsive navigation and graceful handling of image or metadata delays.
  ⦁	Consistent behaviour across supported screen sizes and Android TV-style remote controls.
  ⦁	No exposure of server URLs, API tokens or technical credentials in normal customer screens.
  8. Technical Integration Requirements
  8.1 MegaOTT Panel API
  The published MegaOTT Panel API uses bearer-token authentication and exposes subscription operations that include creating, retrieving, extending, deactivating and activating subscriptions. Gorilla 4444 should place all calls to this API through the secure backend; the token must never be embedded in the customer application or browser code.
  Integration Action	Gorilla 4444 Use
  Identify account / credit	Retrieve the authenticated Panel user and available credit where required.
  Create subscription	Create the appropriate M3U, MAG or Enigma subscription using mapped package and device data.
  Retrieve subscription	Confirm status, package, credentials and expiry information.
  Extend subscription	Apply the mapped package after a verified recurring payment.
  Deactivate subscription	Stop service according to payment or administrative rules.
  Activate subscription	Restore a previously deactivated subscription.
  
  The exact field mapping must be confirmed during the API proof-of-concept. Particular attention is required for device type, MAC address or generated username, package ID, template ID, maximum connections, country setting, adult-content setting, VPN option, paid status and expiry response.
  8.2 PayPal Recurring Payments
  The payment integration should use PayPal Subscriptions for recurring billing. Activation must be driven by a verified server-to-server event, not merely by the customer returning to a success page. PayPal publishes webhook events for successful subscription payments, failed payments, cancellation, suspension, expiry, refunds and reversals.
  ⦁	Verify the authenticity of every webhook before changing service status.
  ⦁	Correlate each PayPal subscription and payment to one Gorilla 4444 customer, device and service subscription.
  ⦁	Activate or extend only after a confirmed successful payment event.
  ⦁	Do not fulfil while a payment remains pending.
  ⦁	Record failed, refunded or reversed transactions and apply the agreed business rule.
  ⦁	Process each webhook idempotently so duplicate delivery cannot cause duplicate fulfilment.
  8.3 QR Pairing and Activation State
  State	Meaning
  Waiting	TV application has created a secure pairing session and is displaying the QR code.
  Scanned	Mobile browser has opened the valid session and claimed the pairing flow.
  Checkout	Package selected and PayPal subscription approval is in progress.
  Payment confirmed	Verified payment event received and fulfilment may begin.
  Provisioning	Backend is creating or extending the service through the MegaOTT API.
  Active	TV device has received valid activation and may open the television interface.
  Exception	Payment or API action failed; no duplicate account should be created and the case must be recoverable.
  
  9. Roles and Access Control
  Role	Permitted Scope
  Platform Owner	Full system configuration, resellers, packages, payments, API integrations, reports and exception handling.
  Reseller	Own customers, invitations, permitted subscription actions, customer records and support notes.
  Customer	Own pairing, package selection, payment, subscription information and television application.
  Support User (optional)	Limited customer assistance without access to payment secrets, master API credentials or unrelated reseller data.
  System Service	Automated payment, provisioning, renewal, suspension, notification and logging processes.
  
  10. Data, Reporting and Audit Requirements
  ⦁	Customer record, reseller ownership and contact information.
  ⦁	Device identifier, platform, application version, pairing sessions and last activity.
  ⦁	Selected package, currency, recurring price and billing frequency.
  ⦁	PayPal customer/subscription/payment identifiers and payment status history.
  ⦁	MegaOTT subscription ID, type, package mapping, expiry and current service status.
  ⦁	Activation, extension, suspension and reactivation history.
  ⦁	Administrative and reseller actions with user, timestamp and result.
  ⦁	API requests and responses logged safely without exposing secrets or unnecessary personal data.
  ⦁	Operational dashboards for active customers, renewals, expiries, failed payments, API failures and reseller performance.
  11. Security and Reliability Requirements
  Area	Requirement
  Credential protection	Store PayPal and MegaOTT secrets only on the server using secure secret management. Never embed them in the TV app or public web pages.
  Authentication	Strong passwords, secure sessions, role-based permissions and optional multi-factor authentication for privileged users.
  Payment verification	Verify PayPal webhook signatures and event status before fulfilment.
  Data protection	Encrypt data in transit, protect sensitive data at rest and minimise personal information collection.
  Idempotency	Ensure repeated requests or webhook deliveries cannot create duplicate customers, charges or subscription extensions.
  Auditability	Maintain immutable or protected logs for payment, provisioning and administrator actions.
  Availability	Use monitoring, alerts, backups and recoverable deployment procedures.
  Error recovery	Retry transient failures safely and place unresolved cases into a visible exception queue.
  Privacy	Provide appropriate privacy notices, retention rules and customer-data handling controls for target markets.
  
  12. Minimum Viable Product and Future Expansion
  12.1 Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
  ⦁	Master administration panel with core platform, package and reseller controls.
  ⦁	Reseller panel with customer creation, invitation and subscription visibility.
  ⦁	Initial television application for the agreed first device platform.
  ⦁	QR pairing and mobile package-selection flow.
  ⦁	PayPal recurring subscription integration.
  ⦁	Automated MegaOTT subscription creation and extension.
  ⦁	Basic suspension/reactivation rules, event logs and administrator exception handling.
  ⦁	Core television interface covering Live TV, Movies, Series, Search, Favourites and Account status.
  12.2 Later-Phase Options
  ⦁	Additional television operating systems and device types.
  ⦁	Alternative payment gateways and local-currency payment options.
  ⦁	White-label branding, domains, applications and payment accounts for other providers.
  ⦁	Advanced reseller billing, credit systems, commissions and multi-level account structures.
  ⦁	Customer notifications by email, SMS, WhatsApp or in-application messaging.
  ⦁	Advanced analytics, churn reporting, recommendations and marketing automation.
  ⦁	Multiple languages, regional packages and market-specific customer journeys.
  13. Recommended Development Stages
  Stage	Purpose
  Stage 1 — Discovery and proof	Confirm target devices, recover any previous assets, validate MegaOTT authentication and package mappings, confirm PayPal account capabilities and prove the complete payment-to-provisioning sequence.
  Stage 2 — Core backend and database	Build customer, reseller, package, device, payment, subscription, webhook, audit and exception services.
  Stage 3 — Master and reseller panels	Deliver secure administration and reseller workflows with role restrictions and reporting.
  Stage 4 — TV app and QR journey	Build the activation screen, pairing logic, mobile purchase pages and the agreed television interface.
  Stage 5 — Automation and failure handling	Complete recurring renewals, suspension/reactivation, retries, reconciliation and support tools.
  Stage 6 — Testing and controlled pilot	Test real devices, payments, duplicate events, interrupted activation, expiry, refunds, failures and application upgrades with a limited customer group.
  Stage 7 — Production launch and handover	Deploy production systems, submit application packages where required and deliver full technical and operational ownership to the project owner.
  
  14. Required Developer Deliverables
  ⦁	Complete source code for all applications, panels, backend services and deployment configuration.
  ⦁	A project repository controlled by the project owner from the beginning of development.
  ⦁	Database schema, API documentation and system architecture documentation.
  ⦁	Build and deployment instructions that do not depend on one individual developer.
  ⦁	TV application installation packages and store-ready builds where applicable.
  ⦁	Testing documentation covering payment, pairing, activation, renewal and failure scenarios.
  ⦁	Administrative user guide and reseller user guide.
  ⦁	Secure transfer of domains, hosting, cloud accounts, application signing keys and third-party service ownership.
  ⦁	A documented backup, monitoring and recovery process.
  ⦁	Formal confirmation that all project-specific source code and deliverables are transferred to the project owner under the agreed contract.
  Ownership safeguard
  The previous project failed without a usable code handover. The new engagement should therefore require frequent repository access, milestone-based source-code delivery and owner-controlled accounts throughout development—not only at the end.
  
  15. Acceptance Criteria
  Test	Pass Condition
  New purchase	A new installation displays a QR code; the customer scans, selects, pays and the same TV device becomes active without manual credential entry.
  Provisioning accuracy	Only one correct MegaOTT subscription is created and it is linked to the correct customer, reseller and device.
  Recurring renewal	A verified recurring payment extends the existing service and updates the displayed expiry information.
  Payment failure	A failed or cancelled payment does not trigger fulfilment and produces the agreed warning, grace or suspension status.
  Duplicate event	Repeated delivery of the same payment event does not create or extend service twice.
  API interruption	A temporary MegaOTT failure is logged, retried safely and remains visible for administrator action without losing payment linkage.
  Reseller isolation	A reseller cannot view or manage another reseller’s customers or platform-level credentials.
  Master control	The owner can configure platform mappings and control resellers, packages, customers, subscriptions and exceptions.
  TV usability	The agreed television screens are readable, visually polished and fully navigable using the target remote control.
  Handover	The owner receives working source code, repository access, credentials, documentation and reproducible deployment instructions.
  
  16. Decisions and Inputs Required Before Development
  ⦁	First launch device platform: for example Android TV / Google TV, Fire TV, Samsung, LG, MAG or another target.
  ⦁	Whether the first service type will use M3U credentials, MAC-based MAG activation or both.
  ⦁	MegaOTT production API token, available package IDs, template IDs and credit rules.
  ⦁	Initial packages, durations, recurring prices, supported currencies and reseller variations.
  ⦁	PayPal business account, application credentials, subscription configuration and permitted card experience.
  ⦁	Brand assets: approved logo, colours, type style, icons, application name and store artwork.
  ⦁	Customer support details and the business rules for failed payments, grace periods, cancellation, refunds and device changes.
  ⦁	Hosting/cloud environment, database preference, domain structure and monitoring arrangements.
  ⦁	Countries, languages and privacy/terms content required for the first launch.
  17. Commercial and White-Label Potential
  The same platform architecture can later support a white-label model. Other television providers could use their own branding, domain, packages, payment account and customer-facing application while the underlying Gorilla 4444 automation engine manages QR pairing, payment events, activation and subscription lifecycle.
  A white-label release should be treated as a separate controlled phase. The data model and configuration structure should nevertheless be designed from the outset so that branding, package mapping, reseller ownership and payment settings are not hard-coded to a single deployment.
  18. Technical References
  The following public technical references were reviewed when preparing this specification:
  MegaOTT Panel API Documentation — authentication, user information and subscription create/retrieve/extend/deactivate/activate operations.
  PayPal Subscriptions — recurring subscription creation and management.
  PayPal Subscription Webhooks — successful payments, failed payments, cancellation, suspension, expiry, refunds and reversals.
  Note: Published third-party APIs can change. The developer must validate the current production documentation, account permissions and test credentials before final architecture and implementation decisions are approved.
