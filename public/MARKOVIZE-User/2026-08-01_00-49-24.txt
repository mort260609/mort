  Class 1: Introduction to AI Video Editing (20-30 Minutes)
  What is AI Video Editing?
  
  Explain:
  
  AI video editing is the use of artificial intelligence to automate tasks like cutting videos, adding subtitles, removing background noise, generating voiceovers, creating animations, and even producing complete videos from text.
  
  Traditional Editing
  
  Edit every cut manually
  Add subtitles manually
  Search music manually
  Remove background manually
  
  AI Editing
  
  Auto cut
  Auto captions
  AI voice
  AI avatars
  AI scripts
  One-click effects
  Why Learn AI Video Editing?
  
  Explain the benefits.
  
  Businesses use it for:
  
  Marketing
  TikTok videos
  Instagram Reels
  YouTube Shorts
  Product ads
  Online courses
  Social media content
  
  Freelancers use AI because it saves hours of editing.
  
  Example:
  
  Without AI:
  3-hour editing job.
  
  With AI:
  20-40 minutes.
  
  Module 2 – Types of AI Video Tools
  
  Tell students there are different categories.
  
  1. AI Video Editors
  
  These edit videos automatically.
  
  Examples
  
  CapCut
  Canva
  Descript
  2. AI Avatar Tools
  
  Generate talking people.
  
  Examples
  
  HeyGen
  Synthesia
  
  Use for:
  
  Explainer videos
  Business videos
  Online courses
  3. AI Voice Generators
  
  Convert text into realistic speech.
  
  Examples
  
  ElevenLabs
  PlayHT
  
  Use for:
  
  Narration
  YouTube videos
  Ads
  4. AI Image to Video
  
  Turn pictures into moving videos.
  
  Examples
  
  Kling AI
  Pika
  Luma Dream Machine
  5. AI Script Writers
  
  Generate scripts.
  
  Examples
  
  ChatGPT
  Claude
  Gemini
  Module 3 – Free AI Tools
  1. ChatGPT
  
  Purpose:
  
  Script writing
  
  Teach them:
  
  Prompt:
  
  Create a 30-second TikTok script promoting wireless earbuds.
  
  Show how it creates:
  
  Hook
  Problem
  Solution
  CTA
  2. Canva (Free)
  
  Explain:
  
  Canva isn't just for graphics.
  
  It now includes:
  
  Video editor
  AI Magic Design
  AI subtitle generation
  Text animations
  Music
  Stock videos
  
  Exercise
  
  Create a Reel inside Canva.
  
  3. CapCut (Free)
  
  One of the best beginner tools.
  
  Features:
  
  Auto captions
  
  Background remover
  
  AI voice
  
  Auto cut
  
  Effects
  
  Transitions
  
  Templates
  
  Color grading
  
  Speed ramp
  
  Teach:
  
  Import video
  
  Auto caption
  
  Add effects
  
  Export
  
  4. ElevenLabs (Free)
  
  Explain:
  
  Generates realistic voices.
  
  Students can type:
  
  "Welcome to my store."
  
  It becomes natural speech.
  
  Uses
  
  Ads
  
  YouTube
  
  TikTok
  
  Courses
  
  5. Pika AI
  
  Turns prompts into videos.
  
  Prompt example:
  
  A futuristic sports car driving through Tokyo at night with cinematic lighting.
  
  AI creates a video.
  
  6. Kling AI
  
  One of the strongest AI video generators.
  
  Can:
  
  Generate videos
  
  Animate photos
  
  Extend videos
  
  Character consistency
  
  7. Luma Dream Machine
  
  Great for cinematic videos.
  
  Prompt:
  
  A drone flying over mountains during sunrise.
  
  Creates smooth cinematic footage.
  
  8. Descript
  
  Amazing for editing talking videos.
  
  Features:
  
  Remove filler words
  
  Delete "um"
  
  Delete "ah"
  
  Studio Sound
  
  Auto captions
  
  Screen recording
  
  Podcast editing
  
  Module 4 – Workflow
  
  Teach this exact workflow.
  
  Idea
  
  ↓
  
  ChatGPT
  
  ↓
  
  Script
  
  ↓
  
  ElevenLabs Voice
  
  ↓
  
  Canva or CapCut
  
  ↓
  
  Add AI captions
  
  ↓
  
  Music
  
  ↓
  
  Effects
  
  ↓
  
  Export
  
  ↓
  
  Upload
  
  This helps students understand how different AI tools work together.
  
  Module 5 – Live Demo
  
  Project:
  
  Create a 30-second product advertisement.
  
  Step 1
  
  Use ChatGPT.
  
  Prompt:
  
  Create a TikTok ad script for a portable blender.
  
  Step 2
  
  Copy into ElevenLabs.
  
  Generate voice.
  
  Step 3
  
  Open CapCut.
  
  Import clips.
  
  Step 4
  
  Add
  
  Transitions
  
  Zoom
  
  Effects
  
  Music
  
  Captions
  
  Step 5
  
  Export.
  
  Show before and after.
  
  Module 6 – AI Prompting
  
  Teach them good prompts.
  
  Bad Prompt
  
  Make a video.
  
  Good Prompt
  
  Create a 20-second cinematic advertisement of a luxury watch with slow-motion shots, black background, dramatic lighting, gold reflections, and emotional orchestral music.
  
  Explain:
  
  The more details you provide, the better the AI output.
  
  Module 7 – Tips
  
  Teach these rules.
  
  Always use:
  
  High-quality footage
  Short clips
  Strong hook in the first 3 seconds
  Captions
  Music
  Brand colors
  Call to Action
  Module 8 – Free Resources
  
  Students always ask where to get media.
  
  Recommend:
  
  Pexels (free stock videos and photos)
  Pixabay (videos, images, music)
  Mixkit (video clips and sound effects)
  Unsplash (high-quality photos)
  Freepik (templates and AI design features)
  
  Explain that these platforms are useful for practice and many commercial projects, but students should always check the licensing terms for their intended use.
  
  Final Class Project
  
  Have each student create a 30–60 second promotional video for a product or service.
  
  Requirements:
  
  AI-generated script
  AI voiceover or their own voice
  AI captions
  At least 3 transitions
  Background music
  Logo or branding
  Call to Action at the end
  
  Afterward, have each student present:
  
  Why they chose their concept
  Which AI tools they used
  What challenges they faced
  One thing they would improve
  
  This reinforces both technical skills and creative thinking.
  
  Homework
  
  Ask students to create two short videos before the next class:
  
  A 15-second motivational video using AI voiceover and captions.
  A 30-second product advertisement using ChatGPT for the script and CapCut or Canva for editing.
  
  This gives them hands-on practice with scripting, editing, and AI-assisted production.
  
  Teaching Tip
  
  Since many beginners learn best by watching rather than listening, keep the class about 20% theory and 80% live demonstrations. As you introduce each tool, open it on your screen, explain what it does, create something in real time, and then let students repeat the same steps. By the end of the session, every student should have completed at least one polished AI-edited video they can share on social media or include in a portfolio.
