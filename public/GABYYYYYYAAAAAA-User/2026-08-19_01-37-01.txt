  Five questions before the build.
  None of these are objections. They are the five decisions that change what gets built, and we
  would rather settle them with you in week one than discover them in week six.
  01 How is a VAT number actually verified?
  Two options and they cost differently. Manual: the admin sees the partita IVA and checks it against the
  Agenzia delle Entrate or VIES by hand before approving. Automatic: the platform calls the EU VIES
  service at registration and shows you a verified or unverified flag. Manual is in every plan. Automatic
  is Plan 2 upward, because it needs error handling for a service that is regularly unavailable.
  02 Is the platform Italian only, or bilingual?
  Every screen, every email and every error message has to exist in whatever languages you choose.
  Italian only is in every plan. Italian and English together is Plan 3, because bilingual is not a translation
  pass, it is a second copy of every string and a language switch that has to survive a page reload.
  03 Who writes the Italian copy?
  You do, or we place what you supply. We write field labels, button text and system messages in
  Italian. Marketing copy, category descriptions and the terms pages are yours, and the platform ships
  with your text or with placeholders you replace.
  04 What happens to a profile when a subscription lapses?
  Your decision, and it is a commercial one, not technical. Hidden from search immediately, hidden after
  a grace period, or left visible but unable to receive requests. We recommend a seven-day grace
  period and then hidden, and page 15 is built that way unless you say otherwise.
  05 Who moderates reviews, and on what rule?
  Reviews are the trust layer, so somebody has to own them. The admin can hide or remove any review
  in every plan. What we need from you is the rule: can a professional reply, can a customer edit, and
  does a review need a completed request behind it.

  § 07 · THE THREE ROLES--------------------
  digitalheroes.co.in  ·  Proposal DH-NIKODINA77-20260819-R1  ·  10 / 36
  Registration and approval, stage
  by stage.
  Nine stages between a professional finding EvaPro and appearing in a search result. Naming them
  separately is what makes the queue debuggable: when somebody says they registered and
  cannot be found, you can say which stage they are sitting in.
  # STAGE WHAT HAPPENS, AND HOW IT FAILS
  01 Registration submitted Email, password, partita IVA, profession, city and service area. The
  profile is created pending and is invisible on the public site. Fails on a
  duplicate VAT number; caught rather than creating a second account.
  02 Email confirmed A link, valid twenty-four hours. Unconfirmed accounts expire and are
  deleted. Fails on Italian providers that strip links; the resend path is built
  for it.
  03 VAT recorded and checked The number is stored and surfaced to you. In Plan 2 upward it is also
  checked against the EU VIES service and flagged verified or unverified. 
  VIES is regularly unavailable, so an unreachable check never blocks the
  queue.
  04 Profile completed Services, description, service area and portfolio. The professional can
  complete this while still pending, so approval is a review of something
  finished.
  05 Admin review You see the profile, the VAT status and the flag, side by side, in a queue
  ordered by how long they have waited. The only stage with no
  automation, deliberately.
  06 Approved, rejected or held Approved goes to payment. Rejected sends a reason you choose from a
  list you control. Held asks for one specific thing and returns to the queue.
  07 Subscription started Stripe checkout at 49.99 euro a month. The profile stays invisible until
  the first payment clears. Fails on a declined card; the professional is told
  immediately and the profile waits.
  08 Published The profile appears in search, on its profession and city pages, and at its
  own indexable URL.
  09 Monitored Subscription state is checked continuously. What happens when it
  lapses is on page 15.
  Stage five is the whole business. Every other stage can be automated and most of it is. The review cannot,
  because the value a customer is paying attention to is that a human checked. The interface for it is built to
  make that check take under a minute rather than to make it disappear.
