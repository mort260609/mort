  # Phoenix Run Collective — Shopify Audit
  
  Reviewed: homepage, `/collections/mens`, a live PDP, Contact, Charitable Partners. This is a rendered-HTML review — I can't test checkout, real device rendering, or Lighthouse numbers from here.
  
  **Verdict up front:** the theme is custom-built and visually ambitious, but it's in a half-finished state with several issues that would block a real launch. This isn't a "polish the animations" job — it's a repair job.
  
  ---
  
  ## 🔴 Critical — blocks launch
  
  **1. Products are priced $0.00**
  6 of 8 men's products show `Sale price $0.00`, and the PDP meta confirms it (`product:price:amount: 0.00`, `availability: in stock`). Customers can add them to cart for free. Only the two RESTOREFLOW/STRIDEFLEX 1/4 zips have real pricing ($44.99).
  
  **2. No policy pages anywhere**
  Footer contains only a "Search" link. No Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Terms, or Refund policy. That's a trust problem, a Shopify Payments / ad-platform compliance problem, and a legal one for a US store.
  
  **3. Duplicate homepage section**
  "SHOP THE PRC COLLECTIONS" and "SHOP THE COLLECTIONS" are two identical collection blocks stacked back-to-back with the same three cards. One is a leftover.
  
  **4. Broken CTA**
  "Learn About the Jocktag" links to an empty `href` — it goes nowhere. The jocktag is the brand's whole differentiator, so this is the most expensive dead link on the site.
  
  **5. Duplicated content on Charitable Partners**
  The V Foundation entry is pasted twice (as items 02 *and* 03 with identical copy), and the entire partner block renders twice in the DOM — likely separate desktop and mobile sections both outputting, with the images dumping as a bare unstyled stack at the page bottom.
  
  ---
  
  ## 🟠 High — product & catalog data
  
  - **Vendor is "Tag Swag"** — the supplier's name is exposed publicly on the PDP and links to a vendor collection. Should be Phoenix Run Collective.
  - **Duplicate/orphaned products.** Handles `...-copy` and `...-copy-1` are live. Worse, the handle `prc-aeroflow™-sunsheild-long-sleeve-hoodie-copy` belongs to a product titled *STRIDEFLEX Mens 1/4 Zip Trainer* — URL and product don't match.
  - **A product with no image.** "PRC RESTOREFLOW MENS 1/4 ZIP PULLOVER" renders as a bare text link in the grid.
  - **™ symbols inside URLs** (encoded as `%E2%84%A2`) on several product handles — ugly, breaks on sharing, bad for SEO.
  - **Typo in a product title:** "SUNSHEILD" → SUNSHIELD.
  - **Contradictory PDP copy:** header says "RECOVER COLLECTION", spec block says "Collection: Core Training | Category: TRAIN".
  - **Variant option rendering is buggy** — Color shows "Navy, Navy, White"; Size shows "XS, XS, S, M…". First option is duplicating.
  - **"You may also like" renders empty** — no related products output.
  
  ---
  
  ## 🟠 High — brand & content
  
  - **Three different brand names in use:** "Phoenix Run Collective" (store name, footer), "Phoenix Running Collective" (hero eyebrow), "Phoenix Running Company" (founder bio, twice). Needs one decision, applied everywhere.
  - **Placeholder asset in production.** The Origin Story image file is literally named `no-it-should-show-a-men-running.png` — a feedback note that became the filename. The intended image was never swapped in.
  - **Contact page is default theme copy** — "Do you have any question?" with a bare name/email/message form. No email, phone, address, response time, or social links.
  - **Homepage has no products on it.** A store homepage that never shows a single product or price is leaving conversion on the floor.
  - **Stat block reads oddly:** "3+ Race Markets" while the section directly below names four cities (NYC, Chicago, Boston, Tokyo).
  
  ---
  
  ## 🟡 Medium — SEO, accessibility, technical
  
  - **No meta descriptions** on homepage, collections, Contact, or Charitable Partners — the og/twitter descriptions just repeat the page title. Only PDPs have real descriptions (auto-pulled from body copy, and truncated mid-sentence).
  - **`maximum-scale=1.0` in the viewport tag** blocks pinch-zoom on mobile — a WCAG 1.4.4 failure and an easy fix.
  - **Missing alt text** on the hero, Origin Story, and all partner images.
  - **`og:image` served over `http://`** while `secure_url` is https — mixed-protocol metadata, poor social previews.
  - **Twitter card is `summary`, not `summary_large_image`** — small thumbnails when the brand is shared.
  - **Doubled DOM output** for nav (header + drawer) and the partner sections increases page weight and gives crawlers duplicate copy.
  - **Newsletter form appears twice** on the homepage with different copy ("Join the Phoenix Running Community" and "JOIN THE PACK").
  - **PDP has three "Add to cart" buttons** in the DOM. Probably a sticky-bar/mobile variant, but worth confirming only one is ever visible and they all fire correctly.
  - **No breadcrumbs, no size guide, no shipping/returns block, no SKU** on the PDP — all expected on a premium apparel store.
  
  ---
  
  ## What I'd want to see before quoting
  
  1. **The design/dev brief they mention** — most of the "is this a bug or a decision?" questions above resolve instantly with it.
  2. **Whether the pricing is a Shopify admin data problem or a theme problem** — if variants genuinely have no price set, that's client-side data entry, not dev work, and should be carved out of scope.
  3. **Theme origin** — custom-coded from scratch, or a heavily modified premium theme? Determines whether fixes are 20 minutes or 2 hours each.
  4. **Admin access level** and whether the previous dev left the theme in a working Git/branch state or only as a published live theme.

  - **Two-stage offer** — a small paid audit/stabilization sprint to hit a launchable state by the 28th, then a second quote for brief-completion once you've seen the brief.
