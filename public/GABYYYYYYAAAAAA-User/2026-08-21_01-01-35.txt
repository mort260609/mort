  Where the sale is lost.
  Somebody lands on a product page from an ad or a link. She has bought supplements before, she
  has seen a lot of stores that look like each other, and she decides in a few seconds whether this
  one is real. Three ways it goes.

  Buys

  ask. Nothing about it says

  Not literally, but the same white

  judgement to the product.

  What Supliful gives you, and
  what it does not.
  Supliful is a good decision. It removes inventory, fulfilment and minimum orders, which is most of
  what stops a supplement brand starting. It is worth being precise about what it leaves for you to
  do, because that is what you are actually commissioning.
  W H A T S U P L I F U L H A N D L E S W H A T I T D O E S N O T , A N D C A N N O T
  The consequence, said plainly. Every Supliful store starts with the same catalogue, the same supplier
  mockups and the same shipping. The only variables left are the brand and the storefront, which means this
  build is not decoration on top of a product business. For the first year it is most of the business a customer
  can actually see.
  None of this is an argument against Supliful. Holding no stock, having no minimum order and not
  forecasting demand is exactly the right way to start a supplement brand, and the alternative would cost you
  thousands before your first sale. The point of this sheet is only that it moves the entire burden of
  differentiation onto the storefront, and you should know that before deciding what to spend on one.

  Five questions before the build.
  None of these are objections. The third decides how the store looks and the fourth decides what it
  may say, so both are worth answering carefully.
  0 1 Is the Supliful account live and are products already synced?
  It changes where we start. If the app is connected and products are pulling through, we work with what

  week of assumptions.
  0 2 Which products, and how many at launch?
  Fewer, presented properly, outperforms a wide catalogue on a new store. Somebody arriving at a
  brand with forty supplements and no reviews does not think depth, she thinks reseller. Our
  recommendation is a tight launch range and adding once something sells.
  0 3 Do you have your own product photography, or only the Supliful mockups?
  This is the question that decides how the store looks, and page 4 explains why. Your own photographs,
  even taken on a phone in good light, will outperform the supplier renders that every other store in the
  catalogue is also using. If you have none, say so and we will build to make the mockups look as good
  as they can.
  0 4 What are you willing to claim about the products, and who is checking it?
  Supplements carry rules about what a label and a website may say. We will write structure, hierarchy
  and benefit-led presentation. We will not write health, therapeutic or outcome claims, and we will not
  invent them from a product name. Send us the wording you or your adviser are comfortable with and
  we will place it exactly.
  0 5 What do you want the store to promise about shipping?
  Supliful fulfils, so their timings and costs are yours to state accurately rather than optimistically. Tell us
  what they quote for your regions and we will put it on the product page, because an unmet delivery
  promise on a first order is the most expensive kind of mistake a new brand can make.
