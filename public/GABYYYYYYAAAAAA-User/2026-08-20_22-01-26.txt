  0 2 What is the royalty actually a percentage of?
  A royalty on a redeemed code needs a base. Ten percent of the discounted order value, a flat amount per
  redemption, or a share of the discount itself are three different commercial deals with three different
  reporting requirements. Flat per redemption is easiest to audit: one number from the supplier, not an order
  value they can shade.

  supplier who is under-reporting will keep doing it because nothing changes.
  0 3 Manual invoicing does not survive twenty suppliers.
  Subscription billing is twenty-six hours and it decides whether your recurring revenue collects itself or
  becomes a monthly job. It also pauses a lapsed supplier's offers automatically, which is the thing you will
  forget to do by hand.

  Owners discussing inspections, staff and incidents will eventually post something that has to come down
