  Main High-Complexity Areas
  
  These are the areas I would emphasize when explaining the price to the client.
  
  1. Course Content Structuring — 🔴 HIGH
  
  The client has 7 educational documents, including:
  
  AI Fundamentals
  AI Marketing & Sales
  AI Automation & Agentic Workflows
  AI Productivity & Business Systems
  AI Business Growth
  SEO Tools & Workflow Lab
  SEO Student Edition
  
  The complexity isn't simply uploading seven PDFs. We need to determine whether each PDF should become:
  
  Academy → Course → Module → Lesson → Quiz → Completion → Certificate
  
  This is one of the largest parts of the project.
  
  2. Automatic Quiz System — 🔴 HIGH
  
  The client specifically requested automatic grading.
  
  We need to configure:
  
  Question → Answers → Correct Answer → Points → Score → Pass/Fail → Course Progress
  
  And then test the entire system.
  
  This is significantly more complex than adding simple interactive questions.
  
  3. Automatic Certificate System — 🔴 HIGH
  
  The certificate needs to be triggered automatically after the required completion criteria are satisfied.
  
  Potential workflow:
  
  Complete Lessons → Pass Quiz → Course Completed → Certificate Generated → Student Downloads Certificate
  
  This needs proper testing because certificate issuance is one of the client's explicit requirements.
  
  4. Dutch Localization — 🔴 HIGH
  
  This isn't just translating the Shopify storefront.
  
  The Dutch experience potentially includes:
  
  Course titles
  Module names
  Lesson content
  Quiz questions
  Quiz responses
  Passing/failing messages
  Progress indicators
  Certificate wording
  Buttons
  Student dashboard
  Navigation
  
  Therefore, Dutch localization adds considerable QA and content effort.
  
  5. Seven-PDF Content Migration — 🔴 HIGH
  
  The biggest unknown is the number of lessons inside those PDFs.
  
  Courses Plus Professional currently has a 100-lesson limit, so we need to structure the content carefully.
  
  For example:
  
  7 PDFs × 10 lessons = 70 lessons → manageable
  
  but:
  
  7 PDFs × 20 lessons = 140 lessons → Professional plan becomes insufficient.
  
  That is why we should not promise an unlimited number of lessons in the base quote.
