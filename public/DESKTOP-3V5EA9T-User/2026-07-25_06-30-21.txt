  How to Use This Document
  This package contains everything the developer needs to rebuild the H2P Solution LLC website on the existing WordPress installation. It is split into two parts:
  •	Part 1 — Design & Build Brief: brand palette, site architecture, page-by-page structure, content rules, the defect fix list, and phasing.
  •	Part 2 — Draft Page Copy: ready-to-place text for every page, including the full service catalog and the program consultation intake form. Items in [brackets] require H2P input before publish.
  
  Guiding principle throughout: the website markets the services but never publishes pricing, curricula, specifications, methods, or customer names. Every price figure lives in the written proposal, not on the site. The website's job is to make a qualified visitor request a program consultation — that consultation is step one of every engagement.
  Company name: always “H2P Solution LLC” — singular “Solution,” no trailing “s.” Apply site-wide, including title tags, footer, and metadata.
   
  PART 1
  Design & Build Brief
  1. Repositioning Statement
  H2P Solution LLC is rebuilding its website around one mission: Counter-Unmanned Aircraft Systems (C-UAS). Prior off-mission content — construction, general IT, generic consulting — is removed. Everything that remains is training, assessment, planning, exercise, and program-development work, presented under the company's operating theme:
  PREDICT.  PREPARE.  PROTECT.
  The site is organized around three customer communities, each with a dedicated page:
  1.	Defense & Intelligence Community
  2.	Public Safety Community
  3.	Foreign Allied Partners
  Services are presented inside a four-stage readiness lifecycle — Assess, Plan, Train, Exercise, Sustain — so a visitor sees H2P can carry them from first assessment to validated, sustained capability.
  2. Brand & Visual Identity
  Match the look of the H2P course materials (Counter-UAS Awareness slide deck). The palette is a near-black navy with gold accents and light-grey text — serious, defense-sector, high-contrast. Use these exact values:
  
  	Hex	Name	Use
  	#0A0D16	Primary background (navy-black)	Page and hero backgrounds, footer, nav bar
  	#0E1420	Secondary navy	Cards, section bands, panels over the base
  	#C9A227	Primary accent (gold)	Buttons, links, icons, key headlines, dividers
  	#A6821A	Deep gold	Accent text on light backgrounds (for contrast)
  	#E7E8EC	Light grey	Body text on dark backgrounds
  	#FFFFFF	White	Primary headline text on dark backgrounds
  
  Typography & treatment:
  •	Clean, professional sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat, Inter, or similar). Headlines in white or gold; body in light grey on dark sections, dark navy on any light sections.
  •	Logo lockup on the slides sets “H2P” in white and “SOLUTION” in gold, with the drone mark in gold. Keep that treatment consistent on the site header.
  •	Gold is an accent, not a flood color — use it for emphasis, calls to action, and iconography. The dominant surface is dark navy.
  •	Maintain the dark-mode aesthetic across all pages; the Public Safety page may lighten cards slightly for readability but stays within the same palette.
  3. Site Architecture
  Home
  ├── Who We Serve
  │   ├── Defense & Intelligence Community
  │   ├── Public Safety Community
  │   └── Foreign Allied Partners
  ├── Services   (Assess → Plan → Train → Exercise → Sustain)
  │   ├── Program Consultation        (entry point / first step)
  │   ├── Assessment & Readiness Planning
  │   ├── Training
  │   ├── Exercises & Validation
  │   ├── SOP / CONOP & Curriculum Development
  │   └── Program Development & Sustainment
  ├── Capability Statement
  ├── About
  └── Contact  /  Request a Consultation   (intake form)
  
  Navigation notes:
  •	Keep the existing WordPress install; retheme to the palette above. Recommend keeping WordPress unless there is a strong reason to migrate.
  •	Delete all construction / infrastructure / generic consulting content and menu items.
  •	“Who We Serve” and “Services” are dropdown menus. Remove FAQ from the nav; fold anything essential into About.
  •	Fix the duplicate navigation menu currently rendering twice on every page.
  •	Primary call to action throughout the site: Request a Consultation → the intake form.
  4. Page-by-Page Structure
  4.1 Home
  •	Hero: Dark navy, C-UAS-thematic, with the PREDICT / PREPARE / PROTECT theme. Single primary CTA — Request a Consultation. Copy in Part 2.
  •	Three-community selector: Three cards (Defense & IC / Public Safety / Allied Partners) — the site's primary navigation device.
  •	Lifecycle strip: Assess → Plan → Train → Exercise → Sustain, one line each, linking into Services.
  •	Services overview: The catalog from Part 2, described by scope and outcome — no prices.
  •	Credibility strip: SDVOSB • CAGE 9BGR9 • UEI R429ZLKG6DA5 • Fairfax, VA. Replaces testimonials.
  •	Footer: Contact block, nav, designations. No newsletter signup.
  4.2 Who We Serve — three audience pages
  Same template, tailored copy: threat framing (2–3 sentences) → what H2P delivers → CTA. Under 200 words each. Full copy in Part 2.
  •	Defense & IC: force-protection framing; .mil/.gov inquiries prioritized.
  •	Public Safety: protector framing for chiefs, security directors, emergency managers; venues and critical infrastructure.
  •	Allied Partners: train-the-trainer emphasis; carries the ITAR/EAR compliance line and the international engagement note; form captures country.
  4.3 Services
  Present the full catalog (Part 2) grouped by lifecycle stage. Each service shows its name, standard starting scope, and outcome — written from the pricing guide but with every dollar figure removed. Each entry ends in the same CTA: Request a Consultation. Include the standing note that final scope and pricing are set in a written proposal.
  4.4 Capability Statement
  Refresh the page to the new palette and offer the one-page PDF (H2P to supply). Ensure the PDF drops any off-mission content so it matches the site.
  4.5 About
  One leadership paragraph, the PREDICT/PREPARE/PROTECT mission in two sentences, and company facts (Fairfax, VA; SDVOSB; CAGE; UEI). Cut generic innovation copy.
  4.6 Contact / Request a Consultation
  This is the most important page on the site. The program consultation is the first step of every client relationship — it is how H2P understands what the client needs. Build it as a TWO-STEP intake (full field lists in Part 2):
  •	Step 1 — short on-site form: point of contact plus community. On submit, the visitor gets a confirmation and an email containing a link to Step 2.
  •	Step 2 — emailed questionnaire: the detailed environment and needs questions, on its own page reachable only via the emailed link, with Step 1 fields pre-filled where possible.
  •	Use a WordPress form plugin with entry storage and email logic (e.g., WPForms, Gravity Forms, or Fluent Forms). Both steps deliver to admin@h2psolution.com and store a copy.
  •	Conditional logic: show the Country field only when “Foreign Allied Partner” is selected.
  •	Each step has a required consent/acknowledgement checkbox; Step 1 also needs anti-spam (honeypot or CAPTCHA).
  •	Display phone and email and city/state only — no street address.
  5. Content & OPSEC Rules
  •	No pricing anywhere on the public site. Services are described by scope and outcome; figures live in proposals only.
  •	No curricula, technical specifications, frequencies, ranges, detection/defeat methods, or performance claims.
  •	No instructor names or unit affiliations; no student or customer photos without written clearance.
  •	No customer names or testimonials. Credibility comes from designations (SDVOSB, CAGE, UEI) and the credibility strip.
  •	The Allied Partners page carries the export-compliance line. Nothing implies availability of controlled items or data to foreign persons.
  •	Every “learn more” routes to Request a Consultation.
  6. Cleanup & Defect Fix List
  4.	Remove any hotlinked / unlicensed stock images (legal exposure) — address immediately, independent of the redesign.
  5.	Delete all construction, risk-management, and generic consulting content site-wide.
  6.	Remove placeholder testimonials and the newsletter signup.
  7.	Fix the “Usefull Link” footer typo and any dead / empty links.
  8.	Remove the duplicate navigation menu rendering.
  9.	Replace the favicon / tile image (currently a PDF-conversion artifact).
  10.	Standardize the company name as “H2P Solution LLC” everywhere (singular).
  11.	Unique page titles and meta descriptions per page (currently all identical).
  7. SEO — Light Touch
  Target terms in titles, H1s, and meta descriptions only; keep body copy natural.
  Page	Target terms
  Training	counter-UAS training, C-UAS training, counter-drone training
  Public Safety	counter-drone training law enforcement, drone threat public safety
  Assessment / Planning	C-UAS readiness assessment, counter-UAS site assessment
  Allied Partners	partner nation counter-UAS training
  Also set up / verify a Google Business Profile with consistent name, address, phone (Fairfax, VA).
  8. Suggested Phasing
