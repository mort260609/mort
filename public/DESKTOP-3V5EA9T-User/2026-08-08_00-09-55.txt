  A Beautiful Slab Is Only Half of a Great Countertop 
  
  Custom countertops succeed or fail in the decisions made after the material catches your eye. Mountain Empire Stoneworks helps you get those decisions right, from planning through templating, in-house fabrication, and installation.

  The Time to Catch a Countertop Mistake Is Before the First Cut 
  
  A small sample cannot show the pattern across an island, the seam location, or the finished edge beside your cabinets. The room adds corners, overhangs, cutouts, wall conditions, and support. 
  
  Those details are easier to settle before fabrication than explain away after installation. 
  
  Slab and layout: When a full slab is available, review its scale and variation before planning the pattern. 
  
  Seams and edges: Discuss visible transitions, edge shape, and thickness before fabrication. 
  
  Sinks and appliances: Confirm models and cutout requirements before templating. 
  
  Site readiness: Prepare cabinets, fixtures, and surrounding work for measurement and installation. 
  
  MES keeps these decisions connected as the project moves from planning to templating, CNC fabrication, and installation. 
  
  The Material Decision Does Not End With Color 
  
  Granite and quartzite vary from slab to slab. Quartz is more consistent and needs less routine care, but it still requires protection from direct heat. Marble etches. Soapstone scratches and changes with use. Porcelain, solid surface, and compact surfaces answer other design and performance needs. 
  
  There is no best surface for every home. The better choice is the one whose appearance, upkeep, and trade-offs make sense for yours.

  A Beautiful Slab Is Only Half of a Great Countertop 
  
  Custom countertops succeed or fail in the decisions made after the material catches your eye. Mountain Empire Stoneworks helps you get those decisions right, from planning through templating, in-house fabrication, and installation. 
  
  CTA 1: Plan My Countertop Project		CTA 2: Call the Nearest MES Location

  The Time to Catch a Countertop Mistake Is Before the First Cut 
  
  A small sample cannot show the pattern across an island, the seam location, or the finished edge beside your cabinets. The room adds corners, overhangs, cutouts, wall conditions, and support. 
  
  Those details are easier to settle before fabrication than explain away after installation. 
  
  Slab and layout: When a full slab is available, review its scale and variation before planning the pattern. 
  
  Seams and edges: Discuss visible transitions, edge shape, and thickness before fabrication. 
  
  Sinks and appliances: Confirm models and cutout requirements before templating. 
  
  Site readiness: Prepare cabinets, fixtures, and surrounding work for measurement and installation. 
  
  MES keeps these decisions connected as the project moves from planning to templating, CNC fabrication, and installation. 
  
  The Material Decision Does Not End With Color 
  
  Granite and quartzite vary from slab to slab. Quartz is more consistent and needs less routine care, but it still requires protection from direct heat. Marble etches. Soapstone scratches and changes with use. Porcelain, solid surface, and compact surfaces answer other design and performance needs. 
  
  There is no best surface for every home. The better choice is the one whose appearance, upkeep, and trade-offs make sense for yours.

  The Room Changes What the Surface Has to Do 
  
  Kitchen Countertops Planned Around the Whole Room 
  
  Cabinets, islands, sinks, cooktops, traffic, and cleanup all affect the material and layout. Plan the surface as part of the kitchen, not an addition at the end. 
  
  CTA: Kitchen Countertops 
  
  Bathroom Vanities Sized Around the Sink, Splash, and Space 
  
  A vanity may use less material, but every inch has a job. The sink, faucet, backsplash, edge, and surrounding walls must work together. 
  
  CTA: Bathroom Countertops and Vanities 
  
  Shower Walls Planned Beyond the Panel
