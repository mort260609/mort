  # Employee Attendance, Time Management & Office Conduct Policy
  
  **Effective Date:** [Insert Date]
  **Applicable To:** All Employees
  **Company:** [Company Name]
  
  ## 1. Purpose
  
  The purpose of this policy is to establish clear expectations regarding employee attendance, punctuality, working hours, time management, office discipline, holidays, overtime, and professional conduct.
  
  Every employee is expected to manage their working time responsibly and maintain a professional working environment while ensuring that client requirements and company operations are not affected.
  
  ## 2. Working Hours
  
  The standard working shift is:
  
  **9:00 PM to 6:00 AM**
  
  Employees are expected to be present and ready to work at **9:00 PM**, not merely arriving at the office at that time.
  
  Employees should plan their commute and personal activities accordingly to ensure timely attendance.
  
  Any changes to working hours or shift schedules must be approved by the relevant manager or management in advance.
  
  ## 3. Punctuality & Late Arrival
  
  Punctuality is an important part of every employee's responsibility.
  
  Employees arriving after the scheduled start time will be considered late. Repeated lateness may result in disciplinary action in addition to applicable salary deductions.
  
  Employees are expected to inform their reporting manager as early as possible if an unavoidable situation may cause them to be late.
  
  However, prior notification does not automatically waive the applicable attendance policy or deduction.
  
  ### Late Arrival
  
  Salary deductions for late arrival will be applied according to the company's approved attendance and payroll rules.
  
  The company reserves the right to take additional disciplinary action in cases of repeated or habitual lateness.
  
  ## 4. Attendance & Time Management
  
  Employees are responsible for accurately recording their attendance and managing their working hours effectively.
  
  Employees are expected to:
  
  * Report to work on time.
  * Remain available during working hours.
  * Complete assigned tasks within agreed deadlines.
  * Avoid unnecessary delays during working hours.
  * Inform their manager about any work-related issue that may affect deadlines.
  * Avoid unnecessary personal activities during working hours.
  * Ensure that client-related tasks are completed within the committed timeline.
  
  Poor time management that repeatedly affects work, deadlines, team productivity, or client commitments may result in corrective or disciplinary action.
  
  ## 5. Leave & Sandwich Policy
  
  Employees must obtain approval before taking planned leave, except in genuine emergencies.
  
  ### Sandwich Rule
  
  If an employee takes leave immediately before and after an official weekly off or holiday, the intervening weekly off/holiday may be counted as leave in accordance with the company's sandwich policy.
  
  For example, if an employee takes leave on **Monday and Wednesday**, with **Tuesday being an official weekly off**, Tuesday may also be counted as a leave day, resulting in **3 days being counted as leave**.
  
  Similarly, where applicable, taking leave immediately before and after an official holiday may result in the intervening holiday being included in the leave calculation.
  
  All leave requests must follow the company's approval procedure.
  
  ## 6. Weekly Off
  
  The company's weekly off will be determined according to the employee's assigned schedule.
  
  Depending on the department or operational requirements, the weekly off may fall on **Monday or Friday**.
  
  Employees are expected to follow the weekly schedule communicated by management.
  
  Any unauthorized absence on a scheduled working day will be treated as absence and may result in the applicable salary deduction and/or disciplinary action.
  
  ## 7. Public & Company Holidays
  
  Employees will receive holidays according to the company's approved annual holiday calendar.
  
  The company will communicate the holiday schedule in advance.
  
  Where business or client requirements require employees to work on an official holiday, the company will communicate the applicable arrangement in advance and provide compensation or an alternative arrangement where required by company policy and applicable law.
  
  ## 8. Overtime & Additional Working Hours
  
  The company recognizes that urgent client requirements or business situations may occasionally require employees to work beyond their normal scheduled hours.
  
  Employees should not be expected to routinely work additional hours without appropriate management coordination.
  
  If an employee is specifically required or approved by management to work additional hours due to an urgent business or client requirement, the additional time will be considered for appropriate compensation or time-off arrangement according to company policy and applicable law.
  
  ### Important Principle
  
  The company believes in fairness and mutual respect:
  
  **Just as employees are expected to respect company working hours and may be subject to applicable deductions for unauthorized lateness or absence, the company will also recognize and appropriately compensate approved additional working hours required from employees.**
  
  Employees should obtain approval from their manager before working additional hours whenever reasonably possible.
  
  ## 9. Dinner Arrangement
  
  The company will arrange a **company-sponsored dinner once every month**, generally on a Friday.
  
  The date and arrangements for the monthly dinner will be communicated by management.
  
  On other working days, employees will be responsible for their own meals and regular dinner arrangements.
  
  Employees are expected to ensure that meal breaks do not unnecessarily affect productivity, client work, or team responsibilities.
  
  ## 10. Office Decorum & Professional Conduct
  
  All employees are expected to maintain professional behavior and a respectful working environment.
  
  Employees must:
  
  * Treat colleagues, managers, clients, and visitors respectfully.
  * Maintain professional communication.
  * Avoid abusive, insulting, threatening, or inappropriate language.
  * Avoid unnecessary arguments or disruptive behavior.
  * Maintain cleanliness and order within the workplace.
  * Respect company property and equipment.
  * Avoid behavior that negatively affects team productivity.
  * Maintain appropriate professional boundaries with colleagues.
  * Follow reasonable instructions from management and reporting managers.
  
  Any behavior that creates a hostile, disrespectful, unsafe, or disruptive workplace may result in disciplinary action.
  
  ## 11. Client Responsibility
  
  Since the company works with international clients and operates according to client schedules, employees are expected to understand the importance of timely communication and delivery.
  
  Employees handling client projects must:
  
  * Meet agreed deadlines.
  * Respond to assigned tasks within reasonable time.
  * Communicate delays immediately.
  * Escalate problems rather than allowing them to remain unresolved.
  * Maintain professional communication with clients.
  * Protect confidential client and company information.
  * Ensure that their absence or lateness does not unnecessarily disrupt client operations.
  
  ## 12. Breaks During Working Hours
  
  Employees may take reasonable breaks during their shift in accordance with company procedures.
  
  Breaks should be managed responsibly and should not interfere with:
  
  * Client meetings
  * Deadlines
  * Team responsibilities
  * Urgent tasks
  * Scheduled calls
  * Project delivery
  
  Employees should coordinate with their team when leaving their workstation for an extended period.
  
  ## 13. Unauthorized Absence
  
  An employee who fails to report to work without prior approval or without a valid reason may be considered absent without authorization.
  
  Unauthorized absence may result in:
  
  * Salary deduction according to applicable rules.
  * Leave adjustment where applicable.
  * Disciplinary action.
  * Further action in case of repeated violations.
  
  ## 14. Repeated Attendance Issues
  
  The company may take progressive disciplinary action when an employee repeatedly violates attendance or punctuality requirements.
  
  Depending on the severity and frequency of the issue, action may include:
  
  1. Verbal warning
  2. Written warning
  3. Attendance-related salary deduction where applicable
  4. Performance or disciplinary review
  5. Further disciplinary action according to company policy and applicable law
  
  ## 15. Fairness & Mutual Responsibility
  
  The company expects employees to respect their working commitments, and the company is equally committed to treating employees fairly.
  
  The company will recognize employees who demonstrate:
  
  * Consistent punctuality
  * Strong time management
  * Reliability
  * Ownership of responsibilities
  * Willingness to support urgent business requirements
  * Professional conduct
  * Consistent contribution to team and client success
  
  Likewise, approved additional working hours required due to genuine business urgency will be considered for appropriate compensation or time-off arrangements in accordance with company policy and applicable law.
  
  ## 16. Policy Compliance
  
  All employees are expected to read, understand, and follow this policy.
  
  Management reserves the right to update or amend this policy when required due to operational requirements, changes in company procedures, or applicable legal requirements.
  
  Employees will be informed of material changes to the policy.
