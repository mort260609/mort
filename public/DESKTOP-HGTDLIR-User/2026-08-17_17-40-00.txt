  56. IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES
  ============================
  
  1. Payment success must be verified server-side.
  
  2. Receipt numbers must be unique.
  
  3. Certificate numbers must be unique.
  
  4. Verification tokens must be unique.
  
  5. Duplicate webhooks must not create duplicate transactions.
  
  6. Blocked members cannot perform restricted member actions.
  
  7. Coordinator can only access authorized coordinator data.
  
  8. Admin has full organization management access according to role.
  
  9. Donor cannot access another donor's information.
  
  10. Beneficiary confidential information must be protected.
  
  11. Deleted records should preferably use soft-delete where audit/history is important.
  
  12. Financial records should not be silently deleted.
  
  13. Every important financial action must create an audit log.
  
  14. Frontend must never contain payment secret keys.
  
  15. Backend must validate all client input.
