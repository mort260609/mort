  - **Theme Variables**: Configured public-only colors in `globals.css` to prevent bleeding into the admin/member dashboards:

  - **Footer**: Rebuilt into four semantic columns displaying dynamic address details, email/phone configs, policy links, and social network shortcuts loaded from MongoDB.
  - **Floating Buttons**: Fixed bottom shortcuts for direct WhatsApp messages and telephone calling, aligned with configured contact information.
  
  ---
  
  ## 4. Pages Redesigned

  - **About (`/about`)**: Grid layout displaying journey summaries, mission statements, and vision.
  - **Management Team (`/management-team`)**: Showcases executive body members in rounded cards with designations.
  - **Membership (`/membership`)**: Displays designation contributions with application forms supporting refer codes.
  - **Donation Page (`/donate`)**: Features secure contribution panel, optional PAN/80G inputs, and quick links to the monthly AutoPay flow.
  - **Campaigns & Details (`/campaigns` & `/campaigns/[slug]`)**: Modern layout showing goals, verified donation totals, donor counts, and progress tracking bars.
  - **Projects & Details (`/projects` & `/projects/[slug]`)**: Displays resource utilization summaries and categorized expense distribution tables.
  - **Events (`/events`)**: Lists camps with registration state tracking.
  - **Internships (`/internships`)**: Displays duration/location eligibility and student application forms.
  - **News & Details (`/news` & `/news/[slug]`)**: Visual press releases and blog posts.
  - **Activities (`/activities`)**: Live feed of ground updates.
  - **Photo Gallery (`/gallery`)**: Reusable masonry grid showing a composite feed of project, campaign, and activity assets.
