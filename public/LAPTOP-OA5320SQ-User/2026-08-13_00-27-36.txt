  TEXT OVER THE IMAGE, OR ABOVE IT
  
  Right now you have both. BEING A LADY sits over the photograph, left-aligned, inside an inset card with margins. MITRA sits centred above a full-bleed image. Two different systems on one page, which is why it feels unresolved rather than wrong.
  
  My recommendation is text below the image, centred, full-bleed, for every section on mobile. Here is the reasoning.
  
  Overlaid type only works when the photograph was art-directed with empty space for it. Yours were not. They are full-frame compositions, beautifully shot, with the subject filling the frame. So on a narrow screen the text lands on the model, the architecture or the balustrade, and your only fixes are darkening the image or adding a scrim. Both cheapen luxury photography, and at this price point that is the one thing you cannot afford.
